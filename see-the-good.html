Dan L'chaf Zechus

Seeing the good in every yid. Every day at camp we watch a clip of someone doing something that looks wrong, and then we stop and work on being Dan L'chaf Zechus.

The Method

How STG Works

Three steps, every single day.

01

Watch

Play the short clip. A yid is doing something that looks like it should not be happening.

02

Be Dan L'chaf Zechus

Before anyone says a word against him, come up with a zechus, a reason the behavior might actually be fine. The harder it looks, the better the answers get.

03

See the Good

Let the video keep playing. The full footage shows what was really going on, and it is almost never what it looked like.
