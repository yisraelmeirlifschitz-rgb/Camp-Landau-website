STG
See the Good

Every day at camp we watch a short clip of someone doing something that looks wrong. Then we stop and ask one question: is there another way to see this?

Once everyone has had a chance to think, we watch the rest of the footage. Almost always, the person was doing something good the whole time. We just could not see it yet.

Episode 1 Today's clip

The short clip plays first. Pause it there and think before letting the rest play.

How we run it

01

Watch the first part

Play the clip and stop when the short version ends. Someone appears to be doing something they should not be doing.

02

Give it a favorable judgment

Before anyone says a word about the person, everyone has to come up with a reason the behavior might actually be fine. The harder it is, the better the answer usually gets.

03

Watch the rest

Let the video keep playing. The full footage shows what was really going on, and it is almost never what it looked like.

Judging someone favorably is a skill. Like any skill, it gets stronger with practice.

That is what STG is for.
