About Camp Landau

Where Every Camper is so GISHMAK

Camp Landau is Beit Shemesh's #1 summer camp. We're packed with Sports, Swimming, Trips, Ruach, Games, Shtick, and SO MUCH MORE!!! Run by Gishmak counselors who live to make every single day insane.
